# B2B Paid Media: Channel Selection & Content Format Guide

## Why B2B Paid Media Is Different

B2B buyers don't make decisions alone. A single deal typically involves multiple stakeholders — a procurement manager, a technical lead, a VP signing off. Each of them may encounter your marketing at different times, through different channels. That's why B2B paid media isn't about driving clicks — it's about reaching the right people at the right funnel stage with the right content.

The common mistake: applying B2C logic (maximize reach, optimize for clicks) to B2B campaigns. B2B requires a fundamentally different approach.

## Channels Overview

### LinkedIn Ads
**Best for:** TOFU and MOFU  
**Targeting:** Job title, company, industry, company size, seniority. Can integrate with ZoomInfo for target account lists — this is what makes it powerful for ABM (Account-Based Marketing).  
**Limitation:** Higher CPCs than other platforms. Not suited for bottom-of-funnel direct response.

### Google Ads
**Best for:** MOFU and BOFU  
**Targeting:** Keyword and search intent. In-Market Audiences and Custom Intent segments let you reach people actively researching a category, but you cannot specify "only CFOs" — that's a key difference from LinkedIn.  
**Use case:** Capturing demand that already exists. If a prospect is Googling "marketing attribution software," you want to be there.

### ZoomInfo Programmatic Display
**Best for:** MOFU  
**Targeting:** Account-level. You upload your target account list and ZoomInfo serves display ads to contacts at those companies across the web.  
**Use case:** Keeping your brand visible to known target accounts between other touchpoints.

### Meta Ads (Facebook / Instagram)
**Best for:** MOFU retargeting  
**Targeting:** Less precise for B2B job titles than LinkedIn, but large audience pool and significantly lower CPCs. Can integrate with ZoomInfo for account-level targeting.  
**Use case:** Cost-efficient complement to LinkedIn for MOFU retargeting. Good for teams with limited budgets who need to maintain presence across multiple touchpoints.

### Content Syndication
**Best for:** TOFU  
**Vendors:** Selling Simplified, Intensify, and similar  
**Use case:** Getting your content (whitepapers, reports) distributed through a vendor's publisher network. You pay per lead. Good for net-new audience reach but lead quality varies — nurture carefully.

### Industry Association / Webinar Partnerships
**Best for:** TOFU → MOFU  
**Use case:** Co-hosting webinars with industry associations or media partners. The value is third-party trust — you reach audiences you can't target directly, with their credibility behind the introduction.

## Content Format by Funnel Stage

The right channel is only half the equation. The content format has to match where the buyer is in their decision process.

| Funnel Stage | Buyer mindset | Channels | Content formats |
|---|---|---|---|
| **TOFU** (awareness) | Not yet actively looking for a solution | LinkedIn, Content Syndication, Webinar | Product video, blog post, whitepaper, thought leadership report |
| **MOFU** (consideration) | Researching and comparing options | LinkedIn, Google, ZoomInfo Display, Meta | Buyer's guide, whitepaper, comparison page, case study, demo invite |
| **BOFU** (decision) | Ready to evaluate vendors | Google Search, Retargeting | Product page, demo request page, pricing page, ROI calculator |

> **Reference:** HubSpot Academy has a free course on the TOFU/MOFU/BOFU framework — [academy.hubspot.com](https://academy.hubspot.com)

## Channel Handoff Logic

Channels should work in sequence, not in isolation.

```
TOFU  →  LinkedIn / ZoomInfo builds awareness
          Accumulates: video viewers, ad engagers

      ↓

MOFU  →  Retargeting on Google Ads, ZoomInfo Display, Meta Ads
          Pushes deeper content to warmed audiences
          Accumulates: website visitors, content downloaders

      ↓

BOFU  →  Google Search captures active intent
          Retargeting reinforces brand at decision stage
```

For more on building the retargeting layer between these stages, see [Retargeting Strategy](retargeting-strategy.md).
